# Rules

We have no interest in ruling this server with an iron fist. Common courtesy will get you far.

**1. Follow Discord's rules**
Discord's [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) apply here too.

**2. Use the server's main language in public channels**
So everyone can follow along and nobody feels excluded.

**3. Respect each other**
Everyone is welcome here. Discriminatory language, targeted harassment and intentionally disruptive behaviour are not allowed.

**4. Respect privacy**
Don't push people for personal information (name, age, location, ID numbers, etc.), and never share someone else's information without their permission.

**5. Uncomfortable DMs**
If someone from the server DMs you and it makes you uncomfortable, take screenshots and report them with `/report @user`, or contact the moderator you're most comfortable with. You can also block them and report them to Discord directly.

**6. No harmful or illegal content**
This covers accounts, posts and reactions: malware, pirated content, exploitative material and anything illegal.
Sexual or sexualised content involving minors, including drawn, fictional or "aged-up" characters, is reported to Discord and results in an immediate ban.

**7. NSFW stays in the age-restricted channel**
That channel is **18+ only**. Moderators may ask you to confirm your age before granting access. No NSFW in avatars, usernames, statuses or any other channel.

**8. Media goes in the media channel**
Elsewhere, only post links, pictures or videos that are part of an ongoing conversation.

**9. No spam or advertising**
This includes posting invites to other servers and advertising to members in DMs without permission.

**10. No impersonation, alts or ban evasion**
Don't pretend to be other members, moderators or public figures, and don't use alt accounts to get around moderation.

**11. No bots**
No bots, self-bots or automated user accounts, except the server's own bots.

**12. Respect moderator decisions**
You may ask another moderator for a second opinion, but follow the original decision until you get an answer.

**13. Ping the moderators only when it matters**
Don't be afraid to ping the moderator role if someone is clearly breaking the rules right now. For anything else, use `/report @user` or right-click a message → **Apps** → **Report message**. Don't ping the moderators as a joke.

**14. Use common sense**
These rules aren't a complete list. Moderators may act on behaviour that clearly goes against their spirit.

## Consequences
Most issues get a friendly word first. Repeated or serious breaks lead to warnings, then timeouts, kicks or bans. Severe violations (illegal content, harassment, raids) can mean an immediate ban.

## Getting help
- **Reports** go privately to the moderators: `/report @user` (you can attach a screenshot) or **Report message** on a specific message.
- **Questions about roles, channels or bots** go to any moderator.
