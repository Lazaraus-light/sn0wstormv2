# Sn0wStormV2 Terms of Service

_Effective 4 November 2026_

These terms cover the use of Sn0wStormV2 ("the bot"), a Discord moderation bot run by its
operator. They apply to anyone who uses the bot's commands and to server staff who add the bot
to a server or configure it. How the bot handles data is described in the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## 1. Discord's rules come first

You must follow Discord's [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and
[Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) whenever you use the bot. Nothing here
overrides them.

## 2. Using the bot

You may use the bot's features as intended. You may not:

- file false, malicious or spam reports, or use `/report` to harass someone
- try to break, overload or get around the bot or its automod, or exploit bugs in it
- use the bot to harass, impersonate or track other people
- try to get access to data or commands you are not authorised to use

Report commands are rate-limited. Misusing them can lead to moderation action in the server
and to being blocked from the bot.

## 3. Automated moderation

The bot removes some messages automatically: invite links, executable files, national ID
numbers, blocked words, mention spam, message floods, and media posted outside the gallery
where the server has asked for that. It can make mistakes. Decisions about members are made
by each server's moderators, who can review and reverse what the bot does. If you think the
bot acted wrongly, contact that server's moderators.

## 4. For server staff

If you add or configure the bot, you are responsible for:

- configuring it to suit your server, including keeping the mod-log and reports channels
  private to staff
- telling your members that the bot moderates the server, for example in your rules
- using moderation records and reports only to moderate your server
- only running `/record start` for a D&D session the people in the channel know about, and
  using recordings only for that campaign's transcripts and notes

The bot only acts in servers it has been added to, and only with the permissions it was given.

## 4a. D&D session recordings

Recording is opt-in per campaign: only the campaign's DM and the players who joined that
campaign with `/dnd` are recorded, and only between `/record start` and `/record stop`. By opting in, you agree to your voice being recorded
in those sessions and turned into a transcript and session notes for the campaign. You can opt
out at any time with `/dnd`.

Don't use the bot to record people who haven't agreed, and don't try to get around the opt-in,
for example by opting in on someone else's behalf. Recordings and notes are for the campaign's
players and DM, not for sharing elsewhere without the people in them agreeing.

every rule break. Features may change, and the bot may be taken offline or discontinued at any
time.
